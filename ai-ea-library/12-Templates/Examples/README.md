# Completed Template Examples

These concise examples show how the templates can be completed consistently. They use one **fictional** scenario: an example retail bank considering a Customer Onboarding Guidance Assistant. They are not operational records, legal advice, compliance certificates, vendor endorsements or reported results.

Dates, costs, scores and thresholds are illustrative planning inputs. An adopting organisation must replace them with approved evidence, assign accountable individuals in its controlled records and obtain appropriate specialist review.

## How to Use the Examples

1. Copy the corresponding template, not the example.
2. Replace every illustrative value with evidence from the proposed system.
3. Preserve unresolved items as open actions; do not turn missing evidence into a positive answer.
4. Use role titles in broadly shared copies and keep personal details only in approved access-controlled records.
5. Treat sign-off as a governance decision, not a guarantee that risk or legal obligations have been eliminated.
