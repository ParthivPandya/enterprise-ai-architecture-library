# India DPDP Act/Rules Alignment Checklist

> Copy this file per AI system that may process digital personal data within the scope of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable Rules. The filename is retained for compatibility.
>
> The Act permits **phased commencement**, including different commencement dates for different provisions. Verify which provisions, Rules, notifications and directions are in force and applicable on the assessment date. The Act does not impose blanket data localisation; notified transfer restrictions and separate sectoral, contractual or other legal requirements may still affect storage, access or transfers.
>
> This is an architecture and governance alignment aid, not legal advice or a compliance certificate. A completed checkbox records that stated evidence was reviewed; completing every check does not prove compliance.

## 0. Applicability and Commencement
- [ ] In-scope processing and territorial applicability assessed
- [ ] Provisions commenced and applicable on the assessment date identified
- [ ] Rules, notifications and directions in force on the assessment date identified
- [ ] Sector-specific, contractual and other legal requirements assessed separately
- [ ] Evidence owner and re-assessment trigger recorded for future commencement or rule changes

## 1. Lawful Processing
- [ ] Lawful basis identified for each category of personal data
- [ ] Consent obtained where required, with clear notice
- [ ] Purpose of processing documented and limited to that purpose
- [ ] Processing limited to data necessary for the stated purpose

## 2. Notice and Consent
- [ ] Notice provided in plain language (and available in applicable languages)
- [ ] Consent is free, specific, informed, and unambiguous
- [ ] Mechanism to withdraw consent is as easy as giving it
- [ ] Consent records retained

## 3. Data Principal Rights
- [ ] Access to information about processing supported
- [ ] Correction and erasure requests supported
- [ ] Grievance redressal mechanism in place
- [ ] Nomination handling supported

## 4. Security and Breach
- [ ] Reasonable security safeguards implemented
- [ ] Personal data breach detection and notification process defined
- [ ] Retention limited; erasure on purpose completion / consent withdrawal

## 5. Cross-Border and Residency
- [ ] No blanket localisation requirement has been assumed
- [ ] Actual storage, processing, support-access and transfer locations documented
- [ ] Restrictions for notified countries or territories checked as at the assessment date
- [ ] Sector-specific localisation, contractual and other applicable restrictions assessed separately
- [ ] Approved residency and transfer conditions captured in the Data Contract(s)

## 6. Significant Data Fiduciary (if designated)
- [ ] Data Protection Officer appointed (India-based)
- [ ] Data Protection Impact Assessment completed
- [ ] Independent data audit scheduled
