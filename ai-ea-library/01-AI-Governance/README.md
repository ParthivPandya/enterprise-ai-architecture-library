# 01 — AI Governance

> *The compliance, risk, and accountability layer. If you deploy AI without reading this section, you are accepting risk you haven't measured.*

This section covers the full governance stack: financial governance (FinOps), regulatory compliance (NIST, ISO, EU AI Act, DPDPA), responsible AI operationalisation, security and risk mitigation, and lessons from real-world AI failures.
