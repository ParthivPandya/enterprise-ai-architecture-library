### Implementing a Specific System

Use this minimum sequence:

1. Complete an [AI Business Case](12-Templates/AI-Business-Case-Template.md).
2. Classify risk using the [AI Risk Classification Framework](09-WG-Outputs/WG1-AI-Governance-Playbook/AI-Risk-Classification-Framework.md).
3. Create an [AI System Card](12-Templates/AI-System-Card-Template.md).
4. Define data with the [Data Contract](12-Templates/Data-Contract-Template.md).
5. Record significant choices in an [Architecture Decision Record](12-Templates/Architecture-Decision-Record.md).
6. Apply testing, monitoring, and red-team guidance.
7. Complete the [Pre-Deployment Launch Gate](12-Templates/Pre-Deployment-Launch-Gate.md).

## Understand Document Authority

Before relying on a page, identify its [document type](DOCUMENT-TYPES.md):

- Normative Framework
- Informative Guide
- Operational Playbook
- Reference Model
- Template
- Checklist
- Worked Example
- Verified Case Study

Normative words in an independent framework express framework requirements. They do not create legal obligations or prove compliance.

## Use Volatile Information Carefully

Model availability, pricing, regulation, and vendor features age quickly. Look for **Last verified** metadata and follow the [Editorial and Citation Policy](EDITORIAL-AND-CITATION-POLICY.md). For legal or standards claims, begin with the [Primary Source Register](SOURCE-REGISTER.md).
