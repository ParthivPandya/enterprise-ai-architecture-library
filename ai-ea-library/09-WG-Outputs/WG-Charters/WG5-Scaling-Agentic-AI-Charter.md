## Scope boundaries

The reference set treats scaling as governed reuse and controlled authority, not as increasing agent count or autonomy. It does not repeat detailed agent engineering patterns from the [Agentic AI Architecture Guide](../../06-Series%20Guide/AIEA-G05-Agentic-AI-Architecture.md).

The TOGAF mapping is independent practitioner guidance and does not claim endorsement by The Open Group.

## Quality criteria

- Scaling decisions preserve purpose, permissions, interruption and accountability.
- Prioritisation keeps exposure separate from enablement.
- Sector indicators are examples rather than benchmarks.
- Every ADM phase has explicit agentic inputs, activities and outputs.
- Architecture links resolve to maintained library artefacts.
