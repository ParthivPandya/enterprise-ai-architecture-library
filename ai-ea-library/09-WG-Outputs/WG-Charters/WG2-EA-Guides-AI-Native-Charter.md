## Scope boundaries

The reference set covers enterprise-level architecture choices and reusable organisational capability. Detailed governance controls remain in [WG1](../WG1-AI-Governance-Playbook/README.md), while agentic scaling concerns remain in [WG5](../WG5-Scaling-Agentic-AI/README.md).

The material is technology-neutral. It does not prescribe a vendor, promise value, certify maturity or imply that every organisation should adopt the same platform model.

## Quality criteria

- Principles include rationale and verifiable implications.
- Architecture components have clear responsibilities and trust boundaries.
- Value measures identify evidence, assumptions, quality and risk.
- Capability choices follow organisational need and dependency rather than maximum autonomy.
- Informative examples remain distinguishable from normative recommendations.
