## Scope boundaries

The reference set supports self-assessment and organisational discussion. It does not provide certification, an audit opinion, a ranking service or a prediction of AI performance.

The method preserves dimension-level evidence and does not let one aggregate number conceal an essential weakness. The roadmap is a capability dependency map rather than a schedule.

## Quality criteria

- The same inputs and rules produce the same heatmap.
- Every score requires evidence and an explicit scope.
- Unknowns and dissent remain visible.
- Colour is never the only information channel.
- Improvement actions follow decision need, root cause and dependency.
