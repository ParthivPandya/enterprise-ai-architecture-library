## Scope boundaries

The reference set:

- provides technology-neutral operating guidance;
- orients readers to NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act and DPDPA subject areas;
- separates normative recommendations from informative explanation; and
- supports organisational tailoring with recorded rationale.

It does not provide legal interpretation, certify compliance, configure a particular product or replace sector-specific professional judgement.

## Quality criteria

- Controls are testable and state the evidence needed.
- External-framework mappings are clearly informative.
- Classification is repeatable from the same facts while preserving professional judgement.
- Checklists expose unknowns and exceptions rather than implying approval.
- No document makes a legal or certification guarantee.
