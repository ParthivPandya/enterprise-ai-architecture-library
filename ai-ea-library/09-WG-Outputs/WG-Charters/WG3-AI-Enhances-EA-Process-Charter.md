## Scope boundaries

The reference set covers AI assistance to architecture practice. It does not transfer architecture accountability to an AI service, automate repository changes without review or treat generated interpretation as legal or policy authority.

Governance control design remains in [WG1](../WG1-AI-Governance-Playbook/README.md). Organisational maturity scoring remains in [WG4](../WG4-Maturity-Assessment/README.md).

## Quality criteria

- Prompts are runnable after filling clearly marked placeholders.
- Factual claims require supplied sources and traceable citations.
- Evaluation includes rejection and escalation conditions.
- Sector examples require local validation and avoid legal guarantees.
- Only reviewed content enters the architecture repository.
