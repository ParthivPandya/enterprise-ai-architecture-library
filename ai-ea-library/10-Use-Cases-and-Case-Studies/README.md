# Section 10 — Use Cases and Illustrative Worked Examples

> Two clearly labelled document types, kept as **standalone, individually citable files**.

- **Reference use cases** are forward-looking 2–4 page scenarios. They test whether a framework or Working Group output is actionable by specifying actors, flows, data, architecture, controls, evaluation and a value hypothesis.
- **Illustrative Worked Examples** are fictional composites used to demonstrate how a rollout might be structured, including approaches that may fail. They are not backward-looking reports, attributed case studies or evidence of achieved outcomes.

Keeping them standalone allows independent review and citation without implying that hypothetical material is empirical evidence.
