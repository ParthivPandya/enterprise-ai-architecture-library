# Submit a Case Study

Use this guidance when proposing an actual, backward-looking case study for Section 10. For a fictional or composite scenario, use the **Illustrative Worked Example** label instead.

## Minimum Submission Content

Provide:

1. **Attribution:** the organisation type and the attribution wording approved for publication.
2. **Permission:** confirmation that the contributor is authorised to share the material and that confidential, personal and security-sensitive information has been removed.
3. **Situation:** the business problem, scope, operating context and relevant constraints.
4. **Approach:** what was implemented, how human accountability operated and which architecture and governance controls were used.
5. **Evidence basis:** the measurement period, baseline, method, sample limitations and source owner for every quantitative claim.
6. **Outcomes:** measured results clearly separated from targets, estimates and interpretation.
7. **What did not work:** material limitations, failed approaches, incidents or changes made during delivery.
8. **Transferable lessons:** what another organisation could adapt and what depends on local context.
9. **AI-ADM mapping:** the phases and architecture artefacts exercised by the work.
10. **Related resources:** relevant library material and any public sources that readers can verify.

## Editorial Requirements

- Use British English and plain, precise language.
- Do not include personal names, handles, credentials, customer data or confidential implementation details.
- Do not present a target, estimate, synthetic test or model-generated value as an observed result.
- Qualify causal claims and state important confounding factors or evidence gaps.
- Do not claim legal or regulatory compliance solely from completed controls or checklists.
- Obtain review from the organisation's authorised legal, privacy, security and communications functions where applicable.
- Make permissions, attribution and evidence limitations explicit in the submitted document.

## Suggested Metadata

| Field | Expected value |
|---|---|
| **Document type** | Case Study (backward-looking and evidence-based) |
| **Evidence status** | Attributed evidence with stated limitations |
| **Sector and geography** | Scope relevant to interpreting the case |
| **Period covered** | Dates or duration of the measured activity |
| **Contributing organisation** | Approved publication name or approved general description |
| **Permission status** | Confirmed before publication |

Submit the proposed Markdown file and supporting public references through the repository's normal contribution channel. Maintainers may request evidence clarification, stronger anonymisation or reclassification as an Illustrative Worked Example before acceptance.
