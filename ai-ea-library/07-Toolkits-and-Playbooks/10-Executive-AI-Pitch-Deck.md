### Core Tenets
1. **Model Independence**: Never lock our business into a single foundation model provider.
2. **Data Sovereignty**: Keep our proprietary enterprise intellectual property inside our secure perimeter.
3. **Reusable Governance**: Shift from slow committee reviews to pre-approved architectural patterns.
4. **Value-Driven Prioritization**: Fund only use cases with clear unit-economic payback within 12 months.

### Speaker Notes
> "This 4-pillar architectural blueprint is our defense against obsolescence. 
> Notice that the foundation model is at the bottom, treated as an interchangeable commodity. Whether OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or open-source Llama leads the frontier tomorrow, our platform architecture remains untouched. 
> By decoupling the application layer from the underlying model layer via an Enterprise AI Gateway, we gain price leverage, mitigate vendor lock-in, and retain 100% ownership of our proprietary knowledge."

- **DPDP Act (India) & GDPR (EU)**: Zero training on enterprise data; automated PII redaction at line rate.
